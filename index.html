Brief Bio-Data

I am a theoretical nuclear physicist working on nuclear structure, weak interaction processes, and large-scale nuclear shell-model calculations. My research focuses on beta decay, nuclear many-body methods, Monte Carlo Shell Model (MCSM), No-Core Monte Carlo Shell Model (NC-MCSM), Quasiparticle-Vacuum Shell Model (QVSM), and ab initio approaches such as the Valence-Space In-Medium Similarity Renormalization Group (VS-IMSRG).

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Center for Computational Sciences, University of Tsukuba, Japan, and a Visiting Scientist at TRIP Headquarters, RIKEN, Japan. I am also exploring Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing applications in nuclear many-body physics.
