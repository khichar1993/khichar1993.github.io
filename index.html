Brief Bio-Data

I am a theoretical nuclear physicist working on nuclear structure, weak interaction processes, and large-scale nuclear shell-model calculations. My research focuses on beta decay, nuclear many-body methods, Monte Carlo Shell Model (MCSM), No-Core Monte Carlo Shell Model (NC-MCSM), and ab initio approaches such as the Valence-Space In-Medium Similarity Renormalization Group (VS-IMSRG).

More recently, I have been exploring Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Quantum Computing applications in nuclear many-body physics, with the goal of developing next-generation computational approaches for nuclear structure calculations..
